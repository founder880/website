# Stacktech Consulting Brand Guide

## Brand Position

Stacktech Consulting is an IT services and consulting partner for growing businesses that need practical technology direction, reliable software delivery, and dependable operational support.

The brand should feel clear, capable, modern, and grounded. It should avoid overpromising, heavy jargon, and flashy startup language. The strongest impression should be: "This team can understand the business problem and build the right technical solution."

## Core Idea

**Practical technology, built for business momentum.**

Stacktech helps companies move from scattered tools, unclear technical decisions, or manual workflows into secure, maintainable, and useful digital systems.

## Audience

- Founders and business owners who need trusted technology guidance.
- Small and mid-sized companies that need software, cloud, data, security, or IT support.
- Operations leaders who want better systems and fewer manual bottlenecks.
- Non-technical decision makers who need technology explained clearly.

## Personality

- Clear, not complicated.
- Confident, not loud.
- Practical, not theoretical.
- Modern, not trendy.
- Reliable, not rigid.

## Brand Promise

Stacktech brings structure to technology decisions and delivers systems that are useful, secure, and maintainable.

## Messaging Pillars

### Strategy First

We help clients understand what to build, why it matters, and how to deliver it responsibly.

### Reliable Delivery

We build software, cloud, and data systems with maintainability, handover, and long-term use in mind.

### Operational Confidence

We support teams after launch with monitoring, maintenance, security improvements, and practical issue resolution.

## Tagline Options

1. Practical technology for growing businesses.
2. IT consulting that turns plans into working systems.
3. Build, modernize, and support better digital operations.
4. Technology strategy and delivery without unnecessary complexity.
5. Reliable IT solutions for business momentum.

Recommended tagline:

**Practical technology for growing businesses.**

It is simple, flexible, and fits consulting, software delivery, cloud, data, security, and support.

## Elevator Pitch

Stacktech Consulting helps growing businesses plan, build, secure, and operate modern digital systems. We combine IT strategy, custom software development, cloud, data, cybersecurity, and managed support to turn business goals into reliable technology outcomes.

## Visual Direction

The visual identity should be clean and structured, with enough warmth to feel approachable.

### Logo Concept

Use a simple wordmark with a compact symbol. The symbol can suggest:

- Stacked layers for systems and infrastructure.
- A connected node structure for technology consulting.
- A subtle upward step for business progress.

Avoid overly complex circuit-board marks, generic globe icons, or abstract gradients.

### Color Palette

Primary:

- Deep Slate: `#0F172A`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Accent:

- Teal: `#0F766E`
- Soft Teal: `#CCFBF1`

Support:

- Slate 50: `#F8FAFC`
- Slate 200: `#E2E8F0`
- Slate 600: `#475569`

Usage:

- Deep Slate for trust, navigation, footer, and strong CTA areas.
- White and Slate 50 for clean content sections.
- Teal for action, highlights, and progress details.
- Avoid making the whole brand blue or purple. The site should feel professional but not generic SaaS.

### Typography

Recommended direction:

- Headings: Inter, Manrope, or Plus Jakarta Sans.
- Body: Inter or system sans-serif.

Style:

- Headings should be clear and medium-weight.
- Body copy should be direct, spacious, and easy to scan.
- Avoid decorative typefaces.

## Tone of Voice

Use plain business language with technical credibility.

Good:

- "We help teams modernize systems without adding unnecessary complexity."
- "Build secure, maintainable software around real workflows."
- "Turn scattered data into dashboards and decisions."

Avoid:

- "Revolutionary digital transformation."
- "World-class cutting-edge solutions for all your needs."
- "Disruptive innovation powered by next-gen technology."

The website should grow into these core pages or sections:

1. Home
2. Services
3. Industries
4. Process
5. About
6. Case Studies
7. Contact

## Service Naming

Keep service names clear and direct:

- IT Strategy and Advisory
- Custom Software Development
- Cloud and DevOps
- Data and Analytics
- Cybersecurity Consulting
- Managed IT Support

## Content Principles

- Lead with business outcomes.
- Explain technical services in plain language.
- Use specific deliverables instead of vague promises.
- Make trust visible through process, examples, and contact clarity.
- Keep the visual system restrained and consistent.
