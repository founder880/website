# Stacktech Consulting Brand Assets

This folder contains the starter visual identity assets for Stacktech Consulting.

## Files

- `public/favicon.svg` - browser favicon using the Stacktech stacked-layer mark.
- `public/brand/stacktech-mark.svg` - primary standalone mark for dark slate backgrounds.
- `public/brand/stacktech-mark-light.svg` - standalone mark for light backgrounds.
- `public/brand/stacktech-logo.svg` - primary horizontal logo.
- `public/brand/stacktech-logo-inverse.svg` - inverse horizontal logo for dark backgrounds.
- `public/brand/stacktech-wordmark.svg` - text-only wordmark.
- `public/brand/stacktech-social-preview.svg` - social sharing preview artwork.
- `public/brand/stacktech-business-card.svg` - editable business card concept.
- `public/brand/stacktech-letterhead.svg` - editable letterhead concept.
- `public/brand/stacktech-palette.svg` - color palette reference sheet.

## Logo Idea

The mark uses three stacked layers to represent technology foundations, systems architecture, and business progress. It is intentionally simple so it works as a favicon, app icon, social avatar, and website logo.

## Usage

- Use `stacktech-logo.svg` on white or light backgrounds.
- Use `stacktech-logo-inverse.svg` on dark slate backgrounds.
- Use the standalone mark when space is limited.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least half the mark width.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, or recolor the logo outside the approved palette.

## Color Palette

- White: `#FFFFFF`
